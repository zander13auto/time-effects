time-effects

Two scroll-driven century timeline effects. Drop-in, dependency-free, designed for editorial and product pages that need to evoke the long view.

Vert-timeline-scroll

A vertical rail pinned to the right edge of the viewport. Decade ticks with absolute year labels; a serif year readout follows the scroll dot.

Horiz-timeline-scroll

A horizontal glass bar pinned to the bottom of the viewport. Centered serif year readout, glowing dot, animated progress underline across decade ticks.
