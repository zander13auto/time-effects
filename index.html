time-effects

Time based
interface tools.

A small collection of standalone HTML files that turn page scroll into a sense of time passing. Drop one into any project, swap the CSS custom properties to match your brand, and replace the dataset.

01 / GRID

52-Week Life Grid

A 52 × 10 decade grid — 520 cells, one per week from 02026 to 02035. Hover any cell for a fisheye magnify that scales upward; click to annotate the week with a note and optional link. Notes persist in localStorage.

--ac · --bl · --sf · --ss Open demo 02 / MAGNIFY

Date Magnify Scroll

A right-edge column of dates drifts past a horizontal lens at viewport center. Whichever date is at the lens scales up, locks gold, and glows; everything else dissolves into the dark above and below. Auto-generates a full year of days; swap the loop for any chronology.

--dms-accent · --dms-serif · --dms-sans Open demo 03 / HORIZONTAL

Horizontal Timeline Scroll

A glass-blur rail pinned along the bottom of the viewport with decade tick labels and a centered serif year readout. Same scroll-driven dot, rotated 90°. Plays well alongside hero video and full-bleed content.

--hts-accent · --hts-bg-tint · --hts-serif · --hts-sans Open demo 04 / VERTICAL

Vertical Timeline Scroll

A fixed rail on the right edge tracks scroll progress as a year-of-100 readout. Decade ticks, glowing dot, serif year display. Best for long-form pages where the destination is "the end of the century."

Using these in WordPress

Each demo is a single HTML file. To embed inside a WordPress block editor page, wrap the markup in <!-- wp:html --> ... <!-- /wp:html --> — without that wrapper, wpautop will inject <br> and <p> tags inside the <script> and break the scroll loop.

Each effect uses its own namespaced CSS custom properties (see the chips on the cards above) so you can include more than one on the same page without collisions.
